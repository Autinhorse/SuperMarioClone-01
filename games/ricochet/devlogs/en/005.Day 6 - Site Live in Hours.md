📅 Devlog #005: Day 6 - Site Live in Hours

Date: May 3, 2026 Hours Logged: 5 Hours (Started at 10:30 AM)

Started the morning by updating the video devlog and journals. Official development kicked off at 14:30. My goal for today was to finalize all planned map elements, but a travel schedule shifted the focus toward the web infrastructure.

1. Progress: Website Launch
Infrastructure:
With the core game mechanics solidified yesterday, I decided to focus on the platform's web presence before heading to the airport.
Looking back a decade ago, building a site meant manually configuring Apache on a virtual machine. Recently, I’ve transitioned to using Vercel and Supabase, where deployment is automated via GitHub. Guided entirely by AI—from account setup to every configuration step—the process has become incredibly efficient.
Starting with nothing more than a reference image, I collaborated with AI to define features and implementation steps. Levelcraft.gg is now live. Total handwritten code: Still Zero.
Note: While many data points are currently placeholders and the Play/Edit modes require direct URL access, launching a functional platform from scratch in just a few hours is a massive milestone.

2. Next Steps: Art & Aesthetics
Tomorrow, once I'm back at my workstation with a proper monitor, I’ll dive into asset creation. The focus will be on "AI Prompt Engineering for Art"—researching how to best direct AI to generate consistent, high-quality game assets.
